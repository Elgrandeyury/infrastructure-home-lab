# Troubleshooting Case Studies

This section will document real infrastructure issues from the lab in a recruiter-friendly format.

For each issue, capture:

## Issue
What failed or behaved unexpectedly.

## Symptoms
What was observed before troubleshooting began.

## Investigation
What was checked, in what order, and why.

## Root Cause
The confirmed cause of the issue.

## Resolution
What fixed the problem.

## Prevention / Lesson
What could prevent the issue from recurring and what the incident taught me.

---

Only real troubleshooting cases should be added. Sensitive addresses, credentials, keys, and personal information should be removed before publishing.
